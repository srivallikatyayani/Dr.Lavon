GLOBAL FIGHT FOR RECOVERY - WEBSITE
===================================

index.html is the complete website. It is a single self-contained file:
all styles, scripts and photos are built into it. To publish, upload
index.html to the root of the hosting (e.g. GoDaddy public_html).

The "assets" folder holds copies of the optimized photos, logo and book
cover in case they are needed elsewhere. The website does NOT need them.

FILL IN LATER
Open index.html and search for "SITE CONFIG" (near the bottom):
  address      - GFR address (shown on the contact section when filled)
  mapEmbedUrl  - Google Maps "Embed a map" link for that address
  heroVideo    - link to the edited black-and-white website video (mp4)
  formEndpoint - backend URL that receives the booking and contact forms
Anything left empty stays hidden. Until formEndpoint is set, the forms
offer to send the details by email to globalfightforrecovery28@gmail.com.

YOUTUBE
The video thumbnail loads from YouTube on the live site. To change the
video, replace the ID KYqdy_kEUeg everywhere it appears in index.html.
